How I work

Principles

What I hold every pipeline to, mine or inherited.

  • 01

    Loads you can rerun

    Every load merges instead of appending, so a retry or a backfill never doubles a number.

  • 02

    Tests that catch real bugs

    I test the failures I've actually seen in production, not just that a column isn't null.

  • 03

    When two numbers disagree, I find out why

    Reconciliation is built into the models, and the rules for which number wins are written down.
